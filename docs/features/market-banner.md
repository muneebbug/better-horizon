# Geo Market Suggestion Banner

The **Geo Market Suggestion Banner** prompts international visitors to switch to their local regional market and currency.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **🌍 International IP Detection:** Uses browser locale / geolocation signals to determine the visitor's country.
- **💱 Single-Click Market Switch:** Seamlessly switches the customer's active Shopify market without confusing redirects.
- **🎨 Sleek Header Integration:** Minimal single-line bar with theme contrast, secondary action button, and dismiss trigger.
- **⚙️ Theme Setting:** Toggle on/off via **Theme Settings > Localization > Enable Geo Market Suggestion banner**.
